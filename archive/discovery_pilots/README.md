# Discovery pilots

These scripts produced the reviewed 92-protein pilot and the broad 10,000-
sequence bacterial pilot. The corresponding large data remain under
`archive/artifacts/discovery_pilots/`.

The reviewed pilot showed identical primary call sets for the structural
detector and fARGene B1 HMM. The broad pilot measured background activation;
because rare B1 ARG prevalence is far below random-protein sampling density,
its zero yield is not evidence that rare environmental B1 enzymes do not exist.
